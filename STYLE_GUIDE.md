# STYLE_GUIDE.md — @suksesishqip Reels

How a Reel is built, beat by beat. BRAND.md says what we look like; this file says how we move; GUIDE.md has the beat types and queue format. Numbers marked (START) are starting values to tune from performance reviews.

## 1. Workflow (do not skip steps)

1. Script: write the voice-over first. Hook, story, payoff.
2. Storyboard: one row per beat with voice line, on-screen text, drawing, motion, sound effect, duration.
3. Check the storyboard against section 6 before building anything.
4. Build scene by scene. Fix one scene at a time; never regenerate the whole video for one bad beat.
5. Render with voice and sound, then run the checklist in section 7.

## 2. Structure

Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Length 20 to 30 s, 5 to 7 beats, one sentence per beat (as in GUIDE.md).

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 2 s | Stop the scroll. Number or relatable situation. Text on screen from frame 1. |
| Stakes | 2 to 6 s | Why this matters to the viewer. |
| Story | 6 s to end minus 6 s | 3 to 5 beats. Each one changes something on screen. |
| Payoff | last 4 to 6 s | The number, the lesson, or the twist. |
| Loop | last 1 s | Last line leads back into the hook. Only if it sounds natural; otherwise end clean. |

Two-part stories: only for gripping ones. Part 1 ends on the open question. Both parts post back to back.

## 3. Hooks

Hooks are the top priority. Two families that work for this page:

- Number hook: a specific figure plus a surprise. Pattern: "[shuma] me [gjë e vogël/e papritur]".
- Situation hook: a moment the viewer has lived. Pattern: "Kur [situatë e njohur]…".

Rules:
- First spoken word lands within 0.3 s. No intro, no logo sting, no "Përshëndetje".
- Hook `big` is a number or 1 to 4 words, in mint with the glow.
- The hook must be true. The video has to pay it off.
- Write 5 hooks per video, pick the strongest, log the rest.

## 4. Motion

- Something changes on screen every 1.5 to 2.5 s (START). Vary beat lengths; equal-length beats feel robotic.
- Elements enter one after another, 3 to 5 frames apart. Never all on the same frame.
- Easing: fast in, soft settle. No linear moves.
- Entrances 8 to 12 frames. Exits faster than entrances, or a hard cut.
- Hard cuts between scenes by default. A transition only when the story changes place or time.
- Numbers count up or slam in. They never fade. The mint glow blooms as the number lands, then settles to a soft halo.
- Every motion explains something or points the eye. Nothing decorative.
- Camera: slow push-in on emotional beats; a quick shake (4 to 6 frames) only on a failure or shock.

## 5. Text and captions

- Captions: word-timed to Riko, 2 to 4 words at a time, in an off-white pill with graphite text; current word in deep green.
- Safe area: keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 420 px (Instagram and TikTok UI).
- Captions sit in the lower-middle third; hook and number text in the upper-middle.
- Never put captions over a drawn face or over other text.
- Check every ë and ç renders. Check every number matches the voice.

## 6. Sound design

- One sound effect per visible movement, synced to the frame the movement lands.
- Music ducks under the voice; rises slightly in the last 2 s.
- Half-second pause in voice and music before the payoff number.
- Peak loudness consistent across videos so the feed sounds like one channel.

## 7. Pre-handover checklist

Watch the full render with sound before handing it over.

- [ ] Hook text visible on frame 1; voice starts within 0.3 s
- [ ] Voice is Riko, excited, 7% faster, no mispronounced words
- [ ] Captions match the voice word for word; ë/ç correct
- [ ] Every number on screen equals the number spoken, and is true
- [ ] No text inside the UI danger zones
- [ ] Sound effect on each movement, none doubled, voice always audible
- [ ] Ending: clean loop or clean stop, no cut-off word, no black frames
- [ ] Colours match BRAND.md; glow on one element per screen at most; no gradient text or same-frame pop-ins
- [ ] "side hustle" left in English

## 8. Learning loop

Every 3-day review, record by format (not by posting hour):

- Which hook family won, with the numbers.
- Retention at 3 s and at the end, if available.
- One change to test next, and the evidence for it.

A style that wins twice goes into BRAND.md section 11 as locked.
