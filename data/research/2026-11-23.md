## A. Labour law and payslip (Kodi i Punës, consolidated text with law 91/2024)
Primary source for all of A: https://idp.al/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Kodi-i-punes-LIGJ-2024.pdf (IDP, uploaded Apr 2025).

### 1. Working on a public holiday; this week's days off `[USED Mon p2]`
- Art. 86: holidays are paid; a holiday on a weekend moves to the next working day. Art. 87(2)–(3): work on a holiday = at least +25% pay AND paid time off equal to the hours, within a week before or after. Work on the weekly rest day = +25% or time off +25%.
- Dates 2026: 22 Nov (Alphabet Day, Sunday) → off Mon 23 Nov; 28 + 29 Nov (Sat, Sun) → off Mon 30 Nov and Tue 1 Dec. https://www.intesasanpaolobank.al/document/documents/ISPALBANIA/Footer-Documents/Bank-Holidays-2026.pdf · https://www.bankofalbania.org/Press/2026_Official_Bank_Holiday_Schedule/2022_Official_Bank_Holiday_Schedule.html
- Honest note: we treat the substitute day (23 Nov) as the holiday for art. 87; that is the plain reading but no ruling was checked.
- Use today: ask in writing how the holiday will be compensated.
- First line: "Punon sot, edhe pse është festë?"

### 2. Minimum wage payslip: the firm pays 58.350, you get 44.400 `[USED Mon p3]`
- Minimum wage 50.000 lekë gross from 1 Jan 2026, 287,3 lekë/hour (174 h): https://shqiptarja.com/lajm/qeveria-merr-vendimin-nga-janari-paga-minimale-rritet-ne-50-mije-leke (19 Dec 2025).
- Contributions: employee 9,5% + 1,7% = 11,2%; employer 15% + 1,7% = 16,7%; tax 0 up to 50.000 gross: https://sherbimekontabiliteti.al/guida/tatimi-mbi-pagen-shkalla-progresive-2025/ (15 Nov 2026) · https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/albania/individual/taxes-on-personal-income (22 Jul 2026).
- Art. 119(1): a written calculation of every payment with all deductions. Art. 118: pay through a bank.
- Our arithmetic: 5.600 contributions → 44.400 net; employer +8.350 → 58.350.
- Use today: ask for the November payslip in writing and check the three lines.
- First line: "Rroga 50.000 bruto: firma paguan 58.350, ti merr 44.400!"

### 3. Overtime +25%, rest day / holiday +50%, max 200 h a year `[USED Tue p3]`
- Art. 91(1)–(3), art. 90(1). Time off instead of pay only if the employee agrees, 25% longer, within 2 months.
- Our arithmetic with 60.000 gross: 345 lekë/h → 431 overtime → 10 h = 4.310.
- First line: "Rroga 60.000 lekë dhe rri pas orarit pa pagesë, kur ligji thotë +25%!"

### 4. Night work 22:00–06:00 = at least +50% `[USED Fri p2]`
- Art. 80, 81(2). (19:00–22:00 +20% only paraphrased: not used.)
- Sendable to: the friend in a bar or a call centre.
- First line: "Punon pas orës 22:00, në bar apo në një qendër thirrjesh?"

### 5. Sick days: employer pays at least 80% for up to 14 days `[USED Wed p3]`
- Art. 130(1). After that social insurance (70% / 80% of the 6-month average by years insured: Alfapress 15 Apr 2025, media only, not used as a figure).
- First line: "Rroga 70.000 lekë dhe sëmuresh tri ditë: shefi s'mund të ta presë krejt!"

### 6. Fines taken from wages are forbidden `[USED Tue p2]`
- Art. 122(3): unless a collective contract allows them.
- First line: "Të mbajtën 2.000 lekë nga rroga se u vonove 10 minuta?"

### 7. Notice when you quit; probation; 20 paid hours to job-hunt `[USED Fri p4]`
- Art. 143: up to 6 months 2 weeks, 6 m–2 y 1 month, 2–5 y 2 months, over 5 y 3 months. Art. 142: probation max 3 months, 5 days' notice. Art. 143(5): when the employer gives notice, at least 20 paid hours a week to look for work.
- First line: "Gjete punë më të mirë dhe do ikësh nesër, pa e njoftuar shefin?"

### 8. Salary at the end of each month, through a bank `[USED Sat p2]`
- Art. 116(1): monthly salary at the end of each month unless agreed otherwise in writing; art. 118(1): through a bank.
- First line: "Rroga e nëntorit të vjen më 15 dhjetor?"
