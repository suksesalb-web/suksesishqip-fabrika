### 2. The raise that lowers your pay: 50.000 → 51.000 gross `[USED Tue p3]`
- Links: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/albania/individual/taxes-on-personal-income (reviewed 22 Jul 2026) · https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf (Art. 22: deduction 600.000 / 420.000 / 360.000 lekë a year = 50.000 / 35.000 / 30.000 a month) · https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/albania/individual/other-taxes (employee contributions 9,5% + 1,7% = 11,2%) · https://shqiptarja.com/lajm/qeveria-merr-vendimin-nga-janari-paga-minimale-rritet-ne-50-mije-leke (minimum wage 50.000 from 1 Jan 2026, 287,3 lekë/hour, about 307.850 employees)
- Rule: wage up to 50.000 → tax 0. 50.001–60.000 → 13% of (wage − 35.000). Above 60.000 → 13% of (wage − 30.000); 23% above 200.000.
- Our arithmetic (net = gross − 11,2% − tax): 50.000 → 44.400 · 51.000 → 43.208 (−1.192) · 52.000 → 43.966 · 53.000 → 44.724 · 60.000 → 50.030 · 61.000 → 50.138 · 80.000 → 64.540 · 100.000 → 79.700 · 150.000 → 117.600. Break-even after 50.000 is 52.572 gross.
- Honest note: the two sources agree on the rule; the net figures are ours. An older article (politiko.al, rules before 2025) shows different bands: ignore it. Ask an accountant to confirm once before the Reel goes out.
- Use today: if you are at the minimum wage and are offered a raise, ask for at least 53.000 gross.
- Slot: MM. First line: "Rroga bruto 50.000 lekë: një rritje 1.000 lekësh ta ul rrogën në dorë!"
