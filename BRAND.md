# BRAND.md — @suksesishqip

Read this file before making any video, carousel or tweet-post. GUIDE.md holds the content rules and the queue format; this file holds the look. If a request conflicts with it, follow the request and flag the conflict.

In the Reel engine this look is `"look": "grafit"` (remotion/src/HypeReel.tsx). The colour roles there map to this table.

## 1. Who we are

- Albanian-language page on side hustles, making money with AI, starting a business, and discipline.
- Audience: as broad as possible. A 19-year-old student and a 40-year-old employee should both get it.
- Promise: concrete, usable, true. Hooks can be a bit clickbaity, never fake.
- Not a news page. No religious, gaming or K-pop content.

## 2. Voice and language

- Albanian, spoken register, short sentences. Talk to one person ("ti").
- Keep "side hustle" in English. Never write "punë anash".
- Numbers over adjectives: "300 € në muaj" beats "shumë para".
- Every claim with a number must be real and checkable. If unsure, drop the number.
- No "swipe", "ndiq faqen" or begging lines on covers. The content pulls the viewer forward.

## 3. Logo

- Wordmark "SUKSESI / SHQIP", white and gold on black.
- Reels: small, top or bottom safe area, max 8% of frame width. Never over the hook text.
- Never recolour, stretch, or add effects to the logo itself.

## 4. Colour

Palette: "Grafit + mint" (chosen 14 Nov 2026).

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Graphite | #1D1E22 |
| Card / panel | Raised graphite | #2A2C31 |
| Primary text | Warm off-white | #F4F1EA |
| Secondary text | Warm grey | #A9A79F |
| Highlight (key word, numbers, wins) | Mint | #3DDC97 |
| Highlight on light surfaces | Deep green | #0E7A4B |
| Alarm (failure, loss, warning) | Coral red | #FF5A4F |
| Logo accent only | Gold | #C9A227 |

Rules:
- Background stays neutral graphite. Never pure black, never a coloured background.
- One highlight colour per screen. Mint OR coral, not both on the same line.
- Coral means something went wrong in the story. Do not use it as decoration.
- No bright yellow. No gradient text, no purple/blue "AI" palette.
- A faint film grain over the background is allowed (barely visible, never a filter look).

## 4b. Depth: shadow and glow

Depth is part of the look. Use it with rules:

- Cards, panels and drawn figures: soft drop shadow, black at 40 to 50% opacity, blurred, offset downward. Short and soft, never hard-edged.
- Hook text: subtle dark shadow so it lifts off the background.
- Glow: mint glow on ONE element per screen, the key number or the winning moment. Coral glow only on the failure beat.
- Never glow on body text, captions, the logo, or more than one element at a time.
- Captions sit in an off-white pill (#F4F1EA) with graphite text; current word in deep green.

## 5. Type

- Scene text: Inter ExtraBold (800), uppercase, tight tracking. Numbers and money: DejaVu Sans Mono Bold. Both ship in `remotion/public/fonts/` and render ë and ç correctly.
- Captions: Inter ExtraBold, sentence case, as spoken.
- Hook text: a number or 1 to 4 words. Readable on a phone at arm's length.
- Numbers are the biggest thing on screen when they appear.

## 6. Voice-over

- Main voice: Riko (Gemini TTS), excited delivery, 7% faster than default.
- Anila only as a last resort if Riko fails.
- Script must be written for the ear: no brackets, no symbols, numbers spelled the way they are spoken.

## 7. Sound

- Music: Pixabay, low under the voice. Voice always wins.
- One sound effect per on-screen movement (whoosh, pop, hit, cash). Never two at once.
- Silence for half a second before the biggest reveal.

## 8. Illustration

- Reels use the illustrated story format: drawn figures and animation, off-white line work on graphite with soft shadows.
- Figures are simple and consistent across a video: same line weight, same proportions, same character from start to end.
- Real people in stories: use photos Mario supplies. Never invent a face or a quote.

## 9. Formats in the mix

- Reels: illustrated story format, 2 per day.
- Tweet-style posts: priority format. Concrete, with numbers, not only inspirational.
- Story carousels (true success/failure stories): 2 per week.
- Side-hustle posts always carry numbers (cost to start, time, realistic earnings).

## 10. Never

- Fake numbers, fake quotes, fake screenshots.
- Copying the look of Plotline.al, millionaire_albania or any other page.
- Stock "AI look": glow on everything, gradient text, everything popping in at once.
- Translating "side hustle".
